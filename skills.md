# Internal Company HRMS - Development Skills & Engineering Rules

## Project Overview

You are building an internal HRMS system for our company.

The system is inspired by:

- Keka HR
- Zoho People
- People HR

But this is NOT a SaaS product.

This system is only for our organization.

Primary goals:

- Digitize HR operations
- Reduce manual work
- Centralize employee data
- Automate attendance and payroll
- Improve employee experience
- Provide management reports

---
