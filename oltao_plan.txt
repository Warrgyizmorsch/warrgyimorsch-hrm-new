1.  Executive Summary
oltao.in runs on a custom Next.js frontend — live, mature, and already selling — sitting on top of a Shopify backend (Shopify-hosted product catalog, cart, checkout and customer accounts at account.oltao.in). The client has asked us to replace that Shopify backend with a fully custom-coded backend portal, built the same way as the Bharat Ceramics ERP: our own admin panel, our own data model, no per-order/per-GMV platform fees, and room to bolt on CRM, inventory, installation, HRMS and marketing automation that Shopify was never going to give them natively.
This document turns the requirements captured in the last client meeting into a phased build plan with epics, in the same format as the Bharat Ceramics plan. Every requirement below is still at Client Review stage — nothing has been costed or contracted yet — so this is a proposal for phasing and scope, not a signed roadmap.
The single biggest decision this plan hinges on is Phase 1: the Shopify-to-custom-backend migration. Everything else — CRM, inventory, installation, automation, customer portal, HRMS — depends on that foundation existing first.

11.  Key Dependencies & Sequencing Logic
Phase 1 blocks everything else — CRM, inventory, installation, portal and HRMS all assume the custom backend (products, orders, accounts) already exists.
Epic 1.8 (Shopify migration & cutover) should be the last thing done in Phase 1, once Epics 1.1–1.7 — including the new COD/partial-payment and tax-invoicing epics — are validated, to minimize downtime risk.
Phase 2's inventory (Epic 2.2) and shipping (Epic 2.5) both carry a wide effort range until the EasyEcom and carrier-list decisions in Section 3 are resolved — worth settling early since they affect Phase 2's overall timeline the most.
Phase 5 (Customer Portal) can start as soon as Phase 1 is live — it doesn't need to wait for Phase 2 or 3, though My Orders (Epic 5.2) gets richer once Phase 2's AWB tracking and Phase 3's installation linkage exist.
Phase 3's VMS integration (Epic 3.4) and Phase 4's IVR/shadow-tracking items (Epics 4.3, 4.5) are the least defined pieces of this plan — they need vendor docs or client clarification before they can be estimated with confidence.
Phase 6 (HRMS) has no dependency on the other phases and could be sequenced earlier if the client wants internal tooling sooner — it's independent of the Shopify migration.
